# Incident Shift Handover — Raw Stakeholder PRD

During follow-the-sun SRE shift rotations, open SEV-1 incidents were accidentally dropped when outgoing engineers signed off before the incoming engineer confirmed ownership.
Build a shift handover validation engine that ensures outgoing and incoming engineers are different people, checks that every open SEV-1 incident is acknowledged, and verifies the incoming responder holds an active on-call certification.
Teams also need a live handover runbook checklist and shadow-shift training.
