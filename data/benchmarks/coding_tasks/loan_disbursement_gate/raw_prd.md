# Loan Disbursement Compliance Gate — Raw Stakeholder PRD

Before wiring consumer or SME loan funds, we must enforce strict KYC and AML sanctions controls.
Never disburse if KYC is incomplete or if there is a sanctions hit.
Loans above $250,000 or borrowers with DTI over 43% must go to a human underwriter rather than automated release.
Compliance officers also follow a quarterly AML audit SOP and refresher certification.
