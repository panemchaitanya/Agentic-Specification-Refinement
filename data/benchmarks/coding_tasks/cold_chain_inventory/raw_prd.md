# Cold-Chain Inventory — Raw Stakeholder PRD

Vaccine pallets in our regional depots must stay between 2C and 8C.
If a pallet freezes below 0C, gets hotter than 15C, or sits out of range for more than an hour, quarantine it right away so it is never shipped.
Shorter excursions should go to QA pharmacists for a stability check. Warehouse handlers also need refresher training on dock-door alarms.
