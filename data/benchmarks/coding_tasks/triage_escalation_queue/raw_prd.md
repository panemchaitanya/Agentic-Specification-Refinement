# Emergency Triage Escalation Queue — Raw Stakeholder PRD

Our ER intake board needs a smarter triage queue so critical patients never get stuck waiting.
Acuity 1 and 2 patients must be routed immediately no matter how full the beds are.
Mid-acuity patients who sit in the waiting room for more than half an hour should alert the charge nurse.
Make sure nurses are trained on the bedside escalation SOP as well.
